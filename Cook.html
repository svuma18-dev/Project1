Briyani

Briyani is a popular rice dish that is loved by many. It is made with aromatic spices and tender meat or vegetables.

To make Briyani, you will need the following ingredients:

  • 2 cups Basmati rice
  • 1 lb chicken or vegetables
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/4 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 4 cups water
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh cilantro and mint leaves for garnish
